## Featured Project

### MLB Pitch Effectiveness — 2026

**Research Question:**
Which MLB pitch types are most effective at limiting offensive production during the 2026 MLB season?

I analyzed **559,722 MLB pitches** from the 2026 season through September 2 using Statcast data from Baseball Savant. The project compares eight major pitch types using run value, whiff rate, wOBA, expected batting average, exit velocity, and hard-hit rate.

**Key Finding:**
Changeups had the highest average Effectiveness Score at **0.713**, while sinkers ranked lowest at **-0.525**.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · Baseball Savant
