## Featured Project

### MLB Pitch Effectiveness — 2026

**Research Question:**
Which MLB pitch types are most effective at limiting offensive production during the 2026 season?

For this project, I used MLB Statcast data from Baseball Savant to analyze eight major pitch types. I created an **Effectiveness Score** using run value, whiff rate, wOBA, expected batting average, exit velocity, and hard-hit rate.

**Key Finding:**
Changeups had the highest overall effectiveness score, while sinkers ranked lowest among the eight pitch types analyzed.
