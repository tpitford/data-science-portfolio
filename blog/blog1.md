What is Data Science to Me?

Data Science to me is the combination of statistics, programming, and problem-solving to turn data into meaningful information. My interest in data science comes from my passion for sports and my desire to understand what the numbers can tell us about performance, strategy, and decision-making. Through my work with Python, R, statistics, and sports analytics, I’ve learned that data science isn't just about analyzing numbers—it’s about asking the right questions and using data to find useful answers.
